# Staff roles and work assignment

Owners configure staff under **Team & access** (`/employees`). Choose **Visa & Ticketing Executive** for a colleague who handles both visa applications and flight/hotel bookings. The role combines the existing Visa Executive and Ticketing Executive operational permissions, including passport-document access. Its record scope is **Assigned**; it grants no finance access, staff administration, delegation, separate work approval or settings management.

Choose the branch whose cases the colleague handles. Active operational staff must have a branch before their profile can be saved. New staff forms initially select the current administrator's branch; existing missing branch selections remain visible until deliberately corrected. The migration adds the role to existing installations but never changes existing staff roles or branches automatically.

In **Work → Add work**, choose the case and service. Assignee options show staff roles and explain unavailable choices such as visa access required or a missing/different branch. The server independently checks role, active status, service and branch on every assignment. Inactive staff are excluded. A separate reviewer must have review permissions and differ from the assignee.

After editing staff in another tab, use **Refresh staff list** in the open work form. Draft instructions, linked records and selected case are retained. A failed refresh keeps the draft. If the selected colleague becomes unavailable, submission is blocked until the coordinator chooses an eligible assignee or explicitly leaves the work unassigned.
