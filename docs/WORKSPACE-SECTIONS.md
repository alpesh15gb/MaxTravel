# CRM workspaces

The primary navigation has six destinations plus a separate Settings utility. Daily work follows a permanent case and named actions; specialist transaction screens remain available inside their section.

| Destination | Entry | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | /dashboard | Agency/branch supervision or staff My day |
| Cases | /cases | First-contact registration and the complete case journey |
| Work | /work | Personal/team work, assignment, waiting, review and outcomes |
| Inbox | /whatsapp | Customer conversations and incoming documents |
| Finance | /workspace/finance | Receipts, invoices, advances, refunds and supplier accounts |
| Reports | /reports/work | Work/team activity, with financial reports at /reports |
| Settings | /workspace/administration | Team/access, templates, agency defaults, checklists and audit |

Section headings navigate; their separate expand controls expose the module links. One group opens at a time. Phone navigation preserves focus trapping, Escape dismissal and focus restoration. Existing workspace aliases redirect to the corresponding destination. Source record screens retain their authorization and show the case context where applicable.

See [Case workflow](CASE-WORKFLOW.md) for staff/admin journeys, reporting bases, permissions, migration and rollout. The [research blueprint](CRM-WORKFLOW-REVAMP-PLAN.md) explains the design decisions. Existing financial transactions and provider outcomes remain authoritative; work status does not move money or confirm bookings.
