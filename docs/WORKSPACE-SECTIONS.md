# CRM workspaces

The primary navigation has five destinations plus a separate Settings utility. Daily work follows a permanent Max ID and named actions; specialist transaction screens remain available inside their section.

| Destination | Entry | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masters | /masters | Customer profiles, travellers, documents, staff activity and customer email |
| Bookings | /ticketing | Travel bookings, online bookings, visa work, Max IDs and customer money |
| Tasks & follow-ups | /work | Personal/team work, assignment, waiting, review, outcomes and daily overview |
| Suppliers | /suppliers | Supplier bills, due dates, payables and settlements |
| Reporting | /reports/work | Work/team activity, with financial reports at /reports |
| Settings (utility) | /workspace/administration | Team/access, agency defaults, checklists, catalogue, imports and audit |

Section headings navigate; their separate expand controls expose the module links. One group opens at a time. Phone navigation preserves focus trapping, Escape dismissal and focus restoration. Existing workspace aliases redirect to the corresponding destination. Source record screens retain their authorization and show the Max ID context where applicable.

See [Case workflow](CASE-WORKFLOW.md) for staff/admin journeys, reporting bases, permissions, migration and rollout. The [research blueprint](CRM-WORKFLOW-REVAMP-PLAN.md) explains the design decisions. Existing financial transactions and provider outcomes remain authoritative; work status does not move money or confirm bookings.
