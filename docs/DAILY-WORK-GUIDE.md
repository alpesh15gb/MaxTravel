# Daily work in Max Travel CRM

## Start the day

Open **Today**. It lists work that needs a decision, with the reason, customer/record and next action. Refresh after resolving an item. Notifications are updates to review; they are not a substitute for completing the underlying task.

Use **Start work** in the navigation whenever you know the task but not its module. Search for payment, booking, visa, invoice or quotation. Only workflows permitted by your role are offered. **Screen guide** explains the current screen in three steps.

## Existing customer

Search for the customer before creating another record. The customer workspace holds travellers, visa cases, travel records, documents and money together. Its related-work links preselect that customer when starting a flight, hotel stay, visa case or statement.

## Quote and sell

1. Start work → Prepare a quotation opens a new enquiry directly.
2. Capture the request, customer, owner and follow-up.
3. Prepare a quote version and review it before publishing its private proposal link.
4. Customer acceptance and creating a CRM booking are distinct steps. A CRM booking alone does not establish supplier confirmation or payment.

## Manual flight or hotel booking

1. Start work → Record a booking / Record a hotel stay.
2. Choose the customer and travellers/guests.
3. Enter the actual itinerary or property/stay dates, source and supplier reference when available.
4. Review price components and known supplier cost, then create the record.
5. Open the record to record issued flight tickets or supplier hotel confirmation. A manual hotel starts as an enquiry even when a reference is entered; confirmation must be explicitly reviewed on its record.

Hotel check-out must follow check-in. A manual stay never reserves a room or calls TripJack. Online transactions remain managed through Online bookings and the booking platform.

## Visa case

1. Select the customer and each applicant.
2. Enter destination and visa type. Choose a matching agency checklist, if configured.
3. Review fees and requirements, then create the case.
4. Review evidence separately for each applicant before moving the application stage. Use the case section selector to reach documents, money or dates directly.

## Customer money

- **Record a customer payment** records money already received; it does not charge the customer. Select the specific invoice, booking or visa allocation. Review the customer, allocation, amount and method before saving.
- **Customer advance / unallocated** means money has arrived before it is allocated. Use Customer advances to allocate that existing receipt later; do not enter a second payment.
- **Create an invoice** creates the document. A general invoice is its own obligation; a linked invoice remains covered by its booking/visa balance. The configured invoice mode controls available operational/tax document options.
- A lost save response must be checked against existing records before creating another entry. Preserve the unchanged receipt form and request reference when retrying a payment.

## Supplier money and refunds

Supplier bills show obligations; settlements show actual payments. Use the supplier statement to inspect the movements behind its balance. Refund requests, approved amounts and completed payouts are different states. Mark returned money only after the payout has actually completed.

## Reports

Reports & insights is directly available under Daily work. Select a report; Browse report library explains each report's question and date basis.

| Question | Report |
| --- | --- |
| What does one customer owe and how did the balance change? | Customer statement |
| What is outstanding or overdue as of a specific date? | Outstanding & ageing |
| What customer cash was received or returned in a period? | Receipts & refunds |
| What does the agency owe one supplier? | Supplier statement |
| What supplier obligations remain open? | Supplier payables |
| Which refund requests remain exposed? | Refund exposure |
| What cancellation charges and cash apply? | Cancellations |
| What sales and known-cost margin are recorded? | Sales & margin |
| What work, ownership and travel deadlines are recorded? | Work & travel |

Choose dates and accounts, then **Generate report**. Editing a filter does not change results until applied. The result shows applied criteria, scope, generation time and source-record links. A copied report-filter link restores criteria; it does not grant access or preserve an immutable data snapshot. Downloads rerun applied criteria and include all matching rows, beyond the visible page. Margin coverage and historical balance limitations are stated on the report; incomplete evidence must not be treated as zero.

## Review the day's transactions

1. Check that money actually received has a receipt, the correct customer and an allocation or explicit advance. Use **Receipts & refunds** for today's cash movements.
2. Resolve unapplied customer advances and review **Outstanding & ageing** for amounts still due. Issuing an invoice is not receiving a payment.
3. Review **Supplier payables** and supplier statements. A supplier bill and its settlement are separate entries.
4. Review refund requests separately from completed payouts. Open the source record for any amount that needs investigation.
5. Inspect sales and work reports for missing costs, owners or deadlines. Save the exported result if you need a dated file; a copied filter link will read the latest records when used again.

These are operational review steps, not a locked accounting-period close. Report scopes follow the signed-in staff member's permissions.
